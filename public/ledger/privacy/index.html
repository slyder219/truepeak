TP Ledger Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026 · TruePeak LLC

This policy covers the TP Ledger app for iPhone and its web version (together, "TP Ledger"). TP Ledger is a bookkeeping tool used by TruePeak LLC ("we") and its clients.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and password. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash.
  • Business records: the invoices, journal entries, balances and client details you or TruePeak enter.
  • Photos and files: receipts or documents you choose to attach, including photos taken with your camera. The app only uses the camera when you choose to take a photo.

We don't collect your location or contacts, and we don't track you across other apps or websites. TP Ledger has no advertising and no third-party analytics.

How we use it

Only to run TP Ledger: to sign you in, show the right client data to the right people, prepare and email invoices, and keep the books. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who processes it

We use a small number of service providers to run TP Ledger, and they handle data only on our behalf:

  • Neon: the database and sign-in service
  • Google Drive: storage for invoices, receipts and attachments
  • Postmark: sending invoice emails
  • Our cloud hosting provider, which runs the TP Ledger servers

We share data with anyone else only if the law requires it.

Retention and deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time. In the app, go to Account and tap Delete account. On the web, use Delete account on your account page. This immediately and permanently removes your login: your name, email, password and sessions.

Invoices and ledger entries belong to the client business's books, not to an individual login, so they are kept after a login is deleted, as bookkeeping and tax records require. To request deletion of business records, email us.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access is limited by role: clients see only their own business's records.

Children

TP Ledger is a business tool and isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Questions or requests: hello@truepeak.us.
