TP Ledger Support

Help for the TP Ledger app on iPhone and the web.

Contact

Email hello@truepeak.us. We usually reply within one business day.

Getting access

TP Ledger is used by TruePeak and its clients. Anyone can create an account in the app, but new accounts have limited access until TruePeak approves them and links them to a client. If you're waiting on access, email us from the address you signed up with.

Common questions

I forgot my password. Email us from your account's address and we'll help you get back in. If you're signed in, you can change your password under Account.

An invoice or balance looks wrong. Email us with the invoice number and we'll look into it.

How do I delete my account? In the app, open Account and tap Delete account. On the web, go to your account page and choose Delete account. Your login is removed immediately. See the Privacy Policy for what happens to your data.
